# Los Cóbanos original-site design

Keep the existing coastal green identity: Inter body text, Playfair Display headings,
real original photographs, rounded cards, direct contact with Victor. This is the
preferred production design; the separate ChatGPT Sites experiment remains optional.

October 2026 refinement: two-column tour comparison, one column below 700px;
full-width bottom-aligned actions; badges in normal flow. Nearby wildlife cards use
the same two-column rhythm with aligned links. Never fix card heights to equalize copy.

Body muted green #477356 is readable on white and pale green. Primary controls use
#1f7a44 with #1a6338 hover; dark section headings remain mint. Keep visible focus
rings, reduce motion when requested, and offset anchors under fixed navigation.

Gallery captions sit below images in normal flow; coffee and sign portraits retain
the full frame. Local images reserve intrinsic dimensions and load lazily below the
hero. Guide heroes are shorter than the homepage opening. Tablet/mobile navigation
keeps section links available in a horizontally scrollable row.

Verify English/Spanish, desktop/tablet/mobile, tour button alignment, portrait captions,
anchor visibility and guide reading. Visual polish does not establish conversion gains.
